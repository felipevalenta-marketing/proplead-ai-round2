# Round 1 decision carried into Round 2

## Decision

**KEEP** the real estate industry and the multilingual lead qualification and property-matching use case.

## Teaching-staff feedback

No major changes were requested. The project should continue with a narrow and reliable MVP.

## Peer feedback

Peers rated the Round 1 presentation **4.00 / 5.00**. They found the multilingual unstructured-to-structured workflow, practical real-estate fit, POC and potential time savings clear. Their main concerns were the limits of synthetic evidence, inconsistent reply language, undefined lead-entry channels, incomplete data, inferred preferences, unavailable properties, usability, rigid classification of edge cases and compliance.

## Round 2 implication

Preserve the Round 1 workflow and human approval gate, but refine the delivery sequence:

1. Make multilingual intake, normalisation, missing-data collection and qualification the core MVP.
2. Run property matching only after the record passes a qualification gate.
3. Block unavailable or stale properties and require human approval.
4. Add measurable regression tests for language consistency, classification, missing data, inference and catalogue availability.
5. Move compliance work earlier.
6. Add an evidence-led agent interface, clarification drafts and a manual-review override for borderline cases.

This is a scope refinement, not a change of industry or primary use case. Automatic sending, CRM write access, negotiation and legal advice remain out of scope.
