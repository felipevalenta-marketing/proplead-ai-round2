# Cost estimate

## Upfront pilot cost

| Item | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Discovery and process mapping | €600 |
| n8n workflow and lead schema | €1,400 |
| Property catalogue and matching logic | €900 |
| Multilingual prompt design and testing | €700 |
| Training, privacy setup and contingency | €600 |
| **Total upfront** | **€4,200** |

## Monthly operating cost after pilot

| Item | Monthly cost |
|---|---:|
| Automation hosting | €60 |
| Model usage for up to 500 enquiries | €40 |
| Monitoring and support | €140 |
| **Total monthly** | **€240** |

## Assumptions

- One agency, one property catalogue and up to five internal users.
- Up to 500 enquiries per month after deployment.
- Existing communication channels remain unchanged during the pilot.
- VAT, paid CRM licences and custom third-party connectors are excluded.
- Final pricing depends on discovery and the systems already used by the agency.
