# Chart documentation

The Round 1 presentation contains five editable charts.

## Chart 1 — Annual house-price growth, Q1 2026

- Purpose: show that the agency operates in a fast-moving, high-pressure market.
- Data: INE House Price Index annual rate.
- Selected values: Spain 12.9%, Illes Balears 13.6%, Madrid 13.6%, Valencia 14.3%, Murcia 15.6%, Aragón 15.6%, Basque Country 10.3%.
- Source: https://www.ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/IPV1T26.htm

## Chart 2 — Foreign share of home purchases, Q4 2025

- Purpose: show why multilingual enquiries matter in the Balearic market.
- Values: Spain 13.5%, Illes Balears 31.47%, Valencia 27.17%, Canarias 23.12%, Murcia 20.74%.
- Source: Colegio de Registradores.

## Chart 3 — AI adoption in Spanish enterprises, 2025

- Purpose: show the adoption gap by company size.
- Values: small 17.2%, medium 30.1%, large 57.5%.
- Limitation: Eurostat covers enterprises with at least 10 employees. Microenterprises are outside the survey scope.
- Source: Eurostat.

## Chart 4 — Monthly enquiry language mix

- Purpose: demonstrate multilingual workload.
- Values: English 24, German 18, Spanish 12, Other 6.
- Data type: synthetic discovery baseline.

## Chart 5 — Missing information in incoming enquiries

- Purpose: identify where qualification creates value.
- Values: financing status 72%, timeline 62%, budget 45%, preferred area 28%, property type 12%.
- Data type: synthetic discovery baseline.

All synthetic figures are hypotheses for the first client meeting and require validation during discovery.
