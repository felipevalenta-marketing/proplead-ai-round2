# Presentation checklist

- Open the PowerPoint and confirm speaker notes are visible in Presenter View.
- Keep the browser demo open before screen sharing.
- Keep the n8n workflow imported as a backup demo.
- Mention that client and operational data are synthetic assumptions.
- Distinguish public market evidence from internal hypotheses.
- Do not claim conversion or ROI improvements yet.
- Ask teaching staff for a clear KEEP or CHANGE recommendation.
- Record feedback immediately in `feedback/round1_decision.md`.
- Confirm which feedback point was repeated most often.
- Identify whether GDPR, ROI or EU AI Act work creates the highest Round 2 risk.
- Confirm that the smallest MVP remains one enquiry to one human-approved response.
