# Sector research

## Scenario

Chleo Realty Mallorca is a fictional independent real estate agency with five employees and approximately 40 active properties. The working baseline assumes 60 buyer enquiries per month through WhatsApp, web forms and email. These figures are discovery assumptions, not measured company results.

## Market context

Mallorca combines high property values with an international buyer base:

- The Spanish Land Registrars reported that foreign buyers represented **31.47%** of registered home purchases in Illes Balears in Q4 2025, compared with **13.5%** across Spain.
- The same source reported an average registered home price of **€4,101/m²** in Illes Balears in Q4 2025, among the highest regional averages in Spain.
- INE reported **13.6% annual house-price growth** in Illes Balears in Q1 2026, compared with **12.9%** nationally.
- Eurostat reported that **17.2% of small Spanish enterprises** used AI in 2025, compared with 30.1% of medium enterprises and 57.5% of large enterprises. Eurostat's business survey covers enterprises with at least 10 employees, so it does not directly measure microagencies.

## Why lead handling matters

An international market increases language variety and makes buyer requirements harder to compare. A microagency has limited capacity to respond quickly across channels, especially when enquiries omit budget, preferred area, financing status or purchase timeline.

## Stakeholders

- CEO / agency owner: wants visibility, control and a realistic cost.
- Sales agents: want faster triage without losing the personal relationship.
- Prospective buyers: expect a relevant answer in their language.
- Data protection lead or external advisor: needs clear data flows and retention rules.
