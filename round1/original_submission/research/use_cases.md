# Use cases

## Prioritisation criteria

Each case is scored from 1 to 5. A higher score is better. Risk uses reverse scoring, where 5 means lower implementation risk.

| Use case | Business value | Feasibility | Data readiness | Lower risk | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Multilingual lead qualification and property matching | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 17 |
| Automated follow-up reminders | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 18 |
| Listing description generation | 3 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 17 |

## Selected use case

**Multilingual lead qualification and property matching** remains the primary use case because it addresses the earliest point in the sales funnel and combines language support, structured extraction and recommendation in one end-to-end workflow.

## MVP capability

Given one free-text enquiry, the system will:

1. Detect English, German or Spanish.
2. Extract budget, location, property type, bedrooms, purpose and purchase timeline.
3. Identify missing information without inventing values.
4. Calculate a transparent lead score.
5. Return up to three compatible properties.
6. Prepare a response for agent approval.

## Out of scope for Round 1

- Automatic outbound messages
- Legal or mortgage advice
- Price negotiation
- Identity verification
- CRM write access
- Recommendations based on protected characteristics
